/how-it-works

State is location

A workspace is a directory. A job is a folder. Its place is its state.

$wrk ./workspace "<prompt>"
$nrvnad ./models/model.gguf ./workspace
$flw ./workspace "$job"

The workspace remembers.
The model does not.

The model is a utility. Your work is central.

The work outlives the process.
