Durable in both directions

  • Answers are files — every completed prompt lands in ~/bckbrnr/text/response/, named after what you asked
  • Failures are files too — a readable .error.txt beside where the answer would have been, not a log line that scrolled away
  • Nothing is lost to timing — answers that finished while the app was closed surface the next time it opens

No chat history, no session, no cloud. The folder is the record.

~/install

Install the developer preview

Download bckbrnr-darwin-arm64.zip for Apple Silicon or bckbrnr-darwin-x86_64.zip for Intel from the latest release. Move the app to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open once.

bckbrnr requires macOS 13.3+ and a local instruct GGUF. It is ad-hoc signed but not notarized. The app bundles the three nrvna binaries; the model is the only separate download.
